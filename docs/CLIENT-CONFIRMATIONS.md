# Client Confirmations

These items must be confirmed before launch.

| Area | Needed Confirmation |
| --- | --- |
| Menu | Final item names, categories, ingredients, dietary labels, and descriptions. |
| Pricing | All item prices and combo pricing. |
| Ordering | Live ordering URL, delivery platform links, or in-house ordering flow. |
| Contact | Phone number, WhatsApp number, email, and preferred contact method. |
| Locations | Store address, map link, service area, and parking/delivery notes. |
| Hours | Opening hours, holiday hours, and kitchen cutoff times. |
| Brand | Final logo files, typography preferences, photography rules, and social links. |
| Legal | Allergen wording, nutrition claims, terms, privacy policy, and copyright details. |
