# Proper Burger Website Re-Audit

Updated: 2026-09-23

## Reference Patterns

The revised experience takes interaction cues from established burger chains without copying their brand presentation:

- Five Guys: direct food-first message, visible ordering action, and a clear path to the nearest location.
- Honest Burgers: easy menu browsing, location-aware information, and clear ways to get a burger.

## Problems Found

1. The previous hero promoted Rollz before Proper Burger, weakening the brand hierarchy.
2. A limited National Cheeseburger Day promotion was presented as permanent pricing.
3. Internal labels such as “Classic Burger Joint” and “Premium Black” appeared in customer-facing areas.
4. Too many repeated sections made the page long without helping a customer choose or visit.
5. The strongest Proper Burger photography was replaced by generic generated imagery.
6. Menu, visit, Instagram, and ordering actions competed instead of forming one clear journey.
7. Mobile users had no persistent menu or directions action.
8. Operational wording from project notes appeared in customer-facing copy.

## Changes Made

- Rebuilt both approved visual options around one concise sales journey: appetite, menu, proof, location, action.
- Restored the supplied Proper Burger food and storefront photography.
- Made Proper Burger the lead brand and moved the Rollz relationship into location context.
- Removed expired promotional prices and dated offer language.
- Reduced navigation to Menu, Why Proper, and Visit.
- Added a direct directions CTA throughout the page and a persistent mobile action bar.
- Added clear Single, Double, and Triple burger cards plus supporting sides.
- Added accurate availability guidance instead of showing unconfirmed prices.
- Added responsive menus, accessible image descriptions, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and a custom favicon.
- Kept a small concept switcher for client review; it can be removed after one direction is approved.
